What each role can do
  • Admin — everything, including users and adding or deactivating stores.
  • Supervisor — all stores; log, edit, resolve and delete issues.
  • Crew lead — only their assigned stores; log, edit and resolve issues there.
  • Pending — signed up, waiting for you. Disabled — no access.
